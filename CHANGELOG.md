# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)
- Project base: Godot 4.7.2 project, placeholder main menu, translations (es, en, pt), GUT tests and CI.
- First person cop (walk, run with stamina, crouch, jump) in a grey test room; Play opens it.
- Grab, carry together and throw physics props; crates (3 kg) and a safe (40 kg, needs two cops) in the grey room.
- Low poly placeholder look: procedural cops, crate and safe from tools/modelgen; colored test room with sky and shadows.
- Pear body for every character: four cops in blue with player skin colors, and ten NPC models (comisario, abuelo, sospechoso, vecina, periodista, asuntos internos, carcelero, preso, abogado, político).
- Fragile evidence: the cookie tin (3 kg, worth 1000) loses value on hits above 3 m/s and breaks at 0.
- Grandpa's house (case 1.1): two floors, 8 rooms, stairs and 10 hiding spots; the tin and the safe hide in a seeded random spot each match. Play opens it; the grey room moved to "Sala de pruebas".
- Locked front door: E searches the pots for the key and opens the door with it; three battering ram hits knock it down (the ram itself comes next). Short hints at the bottom of the screen.
- Battering ram: E picks it up in front of the house; hold left click to charge, let go to hit. Three charged hits knock the door down, a miss staggers the cop, and carrying it slows the walk.
- 147 fragile ornaments (figurines, plates and vases) on shelves, the dining table and the kitchen counter; any real hit shatters one and costs a fine of 20.
- The grandpa: follows the nearest cop around the house on a baked navigation mesh, complains out loud, and fills the complaint meter at the top of the screen; at 100 he calls his lawyer and the case is lost.
- Holding E on the grandpa makes him tell a long story: he stands still for up to 20 s (30 s rest between stories) and the listening cop can't do anything else.
- Patrol car in front of the grandpa's house: E gets in the seat by the nearest door (front left drives) and out again; the driver drives with WASD, Space handbrake and H siren, up to 60 km/h. Low poly placeholder model from tools/modelgen.
- Fix: walls with two doors no longer cover the first one; the living room, the bathroom and the upstairs west rooms were sealed, so the grandpa was stuck in the living room.
- Patrol car trunk: evidence dropped in it counts as delivered (taking it out or breaking it undoes that). The tin in the trunk with every cop aboard solves the case; a broken tin fails it.
- G drops the battering ram (E picks it up again), and the key for what you can do shows under the crosshair.
- End of shift screen: case result, salary, evidence, fines, total against the 800 quota ("¡Turno aprobado!" or "Suspendidos") and three joke awards; "Otro turno" restarts and "Volver al menú" goes back.
- Design docs: decisions, case catalog and working rules.
- The comisario gives case 1.1 (an illegal bingo) before the raid, and Q opens the case checklist; the safe now counts as evidence worth 600.
- The grandpa's house is twice as big: 24 x 20 m and 14 rooms, 12 hiding spots and about 200 ornaments.
- Dressers with drawers you open by grabbing and pulling back slowly; yanked, a drawer comes off and spills what's inside.
- The case is now the grandpa's illegal bingo: find the betting book, plus the bingo cage, the safe with the takings and five bingo cards hidden in drawers.
- Clues for the bingo evidence: the grandpa's notes (E to read), mud footprints and cookie crumbs; found clues go to the Q checklist.
- The safe has a combination, written on the kitchen calendar and inside the cookie tin by the safe; opened, the takings come out as a light cash bundle.
- R questions the grandpa: three answers per case, each one lets slip a hiding spot and annoys him a bit.
- Michi, the grandpa's cat, sleeps on the betting book: picked up he meows and wakes, then weaves between the cops' legs and trips anyone running past; the grandpa hears him and complains.
- The vecina chismosa watches from the house next door: bring her the tupperware the grandpa kept (in a drawer) and she tells where she saw him hide something.
- First person hands: the player sees blue sleeves and mittens in their skin color; they sway while walking, reach for what you hold and hide behind the battering ram.
- Raid stories as data: the bingo (briefing, evidence and drawer cards) moved from the house code to a CaseStory resource, with no change in play. It is the first step to draw a different story each match.
- The house draws its story from the seed (or `--story=<id>`), and the bingo's own pieces (safe combination, crumbs, cards) live in a story rig, so a new story only brings its own.
- Second story for the grandpa's house, the turtle races: catch Relámpago (the turtle with a 7 on her shell) among four lookalikes that roam the house, following lettuce leaves from the kitchen; the betting board and three trophies are extras. `--story=turtles` forces it.
- Guess the story: the comisario only brings an anonymous tip, and the checklist lists three suspicions. Each one has a clue in the house; with Q open, hold 1, 2 or 3 to mark one. A right guess makes the comisario tell what to bring, one wrong guess halves the salary, and two fail the case.
- Design: the open city where every case has its own place and character (docs/diseno/ciudad.md, decision 82).
- City, first blocks: 7 x 5 blocks (about 520 x 370 m) with streets, the police station, a plaza and the closed fronts of the 10 raid places with their signs, plus filler houses and warehouses. The patrol car drives all of it; "Ciudad (prueba)" in the menu opens it.
- The grandpa's case happens in the city: the shift starts at the station by the patrol car, the house sits on its lot among the neighbors, and the case closes when everyone drives back to the station with the evidence.
- City map on the radio: M opens a top view with the station, today's case blinking, the cops as arrows and the patrol car.
- Design: the bakery, second place to raid (docs/diseno/panaderia.md).
- The bakery, inside: shop with counter, cake display and bread shelves (open, shutter half up), locked bakehouse and back door, storeroom with the stairs, bathroom, and Marta's flat with two windows over the street; 12 hiding spots, on its lot in the city. "Panadería (prueba)" in the menu opens it.
- Marta and her customers: customers walk in, wait at the counter and leave once served; Marta works the till or guards the bakehouse door, offers free samples (E: 3 s without grabbing, calms her) and fills the complaint meter. Bumping anyone or ramming a door scares every customer out without paying.
